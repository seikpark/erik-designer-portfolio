Hi, I'm Erik Park

Master's in Service Design Korea's largest design community operator

I am a Product Manager and Product Designer from South Korea, based in Vancouver, BC. I work across product strategy, UX design, AI product planning, and delivery systems to turn unclear problems into practical services.

What I Do

I design digital products, service strategies, and scalable user experiences that connect user needs, business goals, and execution.

With 7+ years of experience across product design, UX strategy, service design, and project management, I have led and contributed to 30+ projects and helped launch 10+ digital services across eCommerce, O2O platforms, healthcare, and AI powered products.

Most recently, I worked at BeroAI, where I contributed to product strategy, UX design, service planning, and user experience development for an AI powered product focused on pet communication.

My Design Toolkit

  • Product Strategy → Turn ambiguous business problems into clear product direction.
  • Service Design → Connect user needs, business models, and operational realities.
  • Systems Thinking → Structure complex services into scalable and usable experiences.
  • Design Management → Align people, processes, priorities, and execution.

What Shapes My Design Leadership

I bring both design depth and product management perspective.

My background in industrial design, service design, and digital product development allows me to move comfortably between research, strategy, UX, execution, and delivery. I understand not only how a product should look and feel, but also how it should work, scale, and create value.

I also bring a global perspective as a Korean designer building my career in Canada. This helps me approach products with cultural sensitivity, practical problem solving, and a strong awareness of how people from different backgrounds experience digital services.

Beyond Design

Beyond work, I stay active as a design mentor, workshop host, side project builder, and community operator.

I mentor designers through ADPList, helping early and mid career designers navigate portfolio development, career direction, product thinking, and the transition between design and product roles.

I also host design workshops and community sessions where designers can exchange ideas, learn practical skills, and build meaningful connections beyond their day to day work.

In addition, I operate one of Korea's largest design communities, creating space for designers to share knowledge, discuss industry trends, and support each other's growth.

Outside of my main work, I continue to build side projects that explore product ideas, AI, service design, and human centered digital experiences.

For me, design is not only about creating better products. It is also about creating better spaces for people to learn, collaborate, and grow together.
