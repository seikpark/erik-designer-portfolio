# BeroAI Case Study

> An AI IoT pet communication service connecting a smart collar, mobile app, and pre order website into one coherent product experience.

## 1. Overview

BeroAI is an AI IoT pet communication service designed to help dog owners better understand their dogs through a smart collar and mobile app experience.

In this project, I worked across service planning, product strategy, mobile app UX structure, product messaging, and pre order website launch. My focus was to connect the smart collar, AI based interpretation, app based insights, and web conversion flow into one coherent product journey.

The core design challenge was not simply to present data collected from a device. The goal was to translate complex signals into clear, understandable, and actionable experiences for dog owners.

## 2. Problem

Dog owners often struggle to understand their dogs’ behavioral changes, emotional signals, stress patterns, and training needs in everyday situations.

Many pet technology products focus on measurable data such as location, activity, or health indicators. However, what dog owners often want to understand is more practical and emotional.

They want to know:

1. Is my dog okay right now?

2. Why is my dog behaving this way?

3. What should I do next?

For BeroAI, the core product problem was to turn signals collected through a smart collar and interpreted through AI into an experience that felt clear, trustworthy, and useful for everyday dog owners.

The project also needed to support early market entry. This meant the mobile app experience alone was not enough. The product also needed a pre order website that could explain the value of BeroAI quickly and guide interested users toward action.

The problem can be summarized into three key questions:

1. How might we turn dog behavior and context signals into an experience owners can understand?

2. How might we explain an AI IoT product through user value rather than technical complexity?

3. How might we connect the mobile app experience and pre order website into one consistent product journey?

## 3. Role & Constraints

My role was to translate BeroAI’s technical product direction into a user centered service experience.

I focused on making sure the smart collar, AI interpretation, mobile app, and pre order website did not feel like separate pieces. Instead, they needed to work together as one continuous journey from product understanding to usage and pre order conversion.

### My Role

**Service Planning**

**Mobile App UX Structure**

The product was in an early launch stage. The experience needed to communicate clear value before long term usage data or mature product metrics were available.

**Trust in AI Communication**  
AI based dog communication can easily sound overpromising. The UX and messaging needed to avoid exaggerated claims and focus on behavior patterns, context, possibilities, and actionable guidance.

**Hardware and Software Integration**  
The smart collar and mobile app needed to feel connected. Device data had to become understandable insights, not isolated technical information.

**Market facing Website**  
The pre order website had to introduce the product, explain its value, and guide users toward pre order action in a short amount of time.

## 4. Solution

The solution focused on turning a technology driven AI IoT product into a clear service experience for dog owners.

### 4.1 Reframing the Product Positioning

BeroAI is powered by a smart collar and AI technology, but the user value is not the technology itself.

For dog owners, the value is being able to understand their dog more easily, make better decisions, and build a stronger relationship through care and training.

Instead of positioning the product as a device that perfectly translates dogs, I framed the product as an AI communicator that helps owners better understand behavior and context signals.

**Core Positioning**  
BeroAI helps dog owners turn behavior and context signals into understandable insights and practical actions for better care, training, and bonding.

**Message Direction**  
Understand your dog better.  
Turn behavior signals into actionable insights.  
A smarter way to care for, train, and bond with your dog.

### 4.2 Structuring the Mobile App Experience

The mobile app was designed as more than a dashboard. Its purpose was to help owners understand their dogs and decide what to do next.

I structured the app experience around three core loops.

#### Understand Loop

This loop helps owners quickly understand their dog’s current status, behavior changes, and possible emotional signals.

Instead of exposing raw data directly, the experience was structured around summary cards, behavior insights, and clear status messages.

**Key UX Elements**  
Dog status summary  
Behavior change alerts  
Emotional signal cards  
Activity and rest pattern summary

#### Respond Loop

This loop helps owners move from insight to action.

When the app identifies a possible issue or pattern, such as anxiety, excessive barking, low activity, or training needs, the experience guides the owner toward practical care actions or training recommendations.

**Key UX Elements**  
Recommended actions  
Training suggestions  
Care guidance  
Context based owner tips

#### Bonding Loop

This loop supports repeat engagement and strengthens the relationship between the owner and the dog.

The experience uses training missions, achievement records, profile growth, and gamified interactions to make the app feel like part of the owner and dog relationship, not just a monitoring tool.

**Key UX Elements**  
Training missions  
Achievement records  
Dog profile growth  
Gamified interactions

### 4.3 Designing the Service Flow

The service flow was structured to make sure the hardware and app experience worked together clearly.

**Service Flow**  
Smart collar  
AI interpretation  
Behavior insight  
Recommended action  
Training or care  
Stronger owner dog bond

This flow helped translate device signals into user facing insights and actions.

## 6. Reflection

This project reinforced that AI product design is not only about showing intelligence. It is about helping users understand, trust, and act on that intelligence.

For a product concept like dog communication, the experience needed to be careful with its claims. Instead of presenting AI as something that can perfectly translate dogs, the service needed to focus on contextual insights, behavior patterns, and practical guidance.

The project also showed the importance of connecting hardware, software, and web communication into one product journey. The smart collar, mobile app, and pre order site each had a different role, but they needed to communicate the same value clearly.

BeroAI became an example of turning an early AI IoT product into a market facing service experience, with a clear product story, app structure, and pre order launch path.
