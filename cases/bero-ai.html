AI-powered dog care and translation app

Led UX strategy, product flows, and market validation for a multimodal AI platform that turns smart-collar behavior data into meaningful care guidance for owners.

Overview

Bero AI needed a product experience that could translate smart-collar behavior data into understandable care moments for dog owners.

Challenge

The core challenge was making AI interpretation feel practical and trustworthy by connecting behavior signals, emotional labels, and owner-facing guidance.

Approach

  • Defined core UX flows across avatar, chat, map, training, and gamified care features.
  • Structured behavior and emotion labels to support AI-driven interpretation and personalized guidance.
  • Developed pre-order pricing and benefit packages for early market validation.

Outcome

The work clarified how multimodal AI features could become a coherent care experience and gave the product a validation path through pre-order packages.
