BeroAI · AI IoT Product by Erik Park · 2025 Q4 to 2026 Q2

Turning smart collar signals into context owners can understand.

BeroAI connects a smart collar, mobile app, and pre order website into one service experience that helps dog owners move from uncertainty to care, training, and bonding.

EPErik ParkProduct Strategy / Service Design / UX Design / UI Design / Character Design / Conversion UX

Overview

BeroAI is a pet communication service built around a smart collar and mobile app. The product does not only track a dog. It interprets behavior signals and turns them into owner friendly messages, care suggestions, training support, and daily records.

My work connected product strategy, service planning, mobile app UX, AI communication logic, pre order website structure, and conversion flow. The main challenge was to make a technical AI IoT product feel clear, trustworthy, and emotionally useful for everyday dog owners.

Area Scope
Product type AI IoT pet communication service
Platforms Smart collar, mobile app, pre order website
Role Product strategy, service design, user flow design, mobile UI design, avatar and character design, product messaging, launch website planning
Core output App IA and user flows, mobile UI screen framework, avatar and character direction, AI communication scenarios, onboarding and state rules, pre order funnel structure

Problem

The hardest part of caring for a dog is often not the care itself. It is not knowing. Owners can see activity, camera footage, or health numbers, but they still need help understanding what those signals mean and what to do next.

Most pet technology products are strong at tracking location, activity, or health indicators. BeroAI needed to go further by explaining context, intent, and care actions without making unrealistic claims about perfectly translating dogs.

Tension Design challenge
Raw data vs meaning Turn barking, movement, location, and routine signals into understandable owner guidance.
AI promise vs trust Communicate possibilities and context without overclaiming that AI can know everything.
Hardware vs service Make the collar, app, training, map, and website feel like one continuous product.
Curiosity vs conversion Explain a new category quickly enough for users to understand and join the pre order flow.

Role and Focus

I worked across the product layer, the app design layer, and the market facing layer. The goal was to shape the service logic behind the product and make that logic understandable through user flows, mobile UI, avatar interactions, product messaging, and the pre order website.

Product Positioning

The product direction had to avoid sounding like magic. Instead of claiming that BeroAI can perfectly translate dogs, I framed it as a communicator that helps owners understand behavior, context, and possible needs.

This made the product more credible. It also gave the mobile app a clear reason to exist beyond data display: helping owners feel more connected, more informed, and more at ease.

Instead of I positioned BeroAI as
A smart collar that tracks activity A service that turns dog behavior signals into context and care guidance.
An AI that "translates" dogs perfectly An AI communicator that helps owners interpret patterns and respond with better timing.
A dashboard of pet metrics A daily relationship tool built around avatar status, chat insights, training, and records.

Service Architecture

I organized BeroAI as a service system, not a set of separate screens. Each touchpoint needed to answer a different part of the owner journey, from product understanding to collar setup, daily interpretation, action, and long term engagement.

Layer Purpose Experience output
Smart collar Collect behavior, motion, sound, location, and routine signals. Raw signals and confidence limits
AI interpretation Turn signal patterns into possible contexts and owner friendly explanations. Behavior insight, reason, intent, suggested action
Avatar and Chat Make the dog's status and context feel immediate and emotionally legible. Avatar state, chat message, tone variation
Map and Training Support outdoor safety, activity records, training practice, and repeat engagement. Walk report, route insight, training journey, missions

AI Communication UX

The Chat Room was designed as a context interpretation interface. A standard dashboard might show a result such as barking, low activity, or location. BeroAI needed to explain the possible reason behind that result and guide the owner toward an appropriate response.

I treated each AI message as part of a scenario loop: trigger, interpretation, owner action, and resolution. In some cases, the system could resolve a scenario without extra app interaction if real world signals changed after feeding, play, a walk, or a hug.

Scenario Signal pattern Owner facing interpretation
Unfamiliar noise Unusual sound, fixed head direction, barking The dog may be alert because of an unfamiliar sound outside.
Object obsession Stays at one coordinate, sniffing, repeated focus The dog may be trying to reach a toy or object nearby.
Activity based hunger High activity, mealtime proximity, continued hunger cues The dog may need food earlier than usual after using more energy.
Separation anxiety Frozen posture near entrance, micro howling, owner away The dog may be waiting anxiously and reacting to outside footsteps.

Care Home Experience

Care Home was designed as the main emotional surface of the app. Instead of making users start from a dashboard, the experience opens with the dog's current state, visible mood, recent context, and the next action the owner can take.

The screen needed to work as both a live status view and a navigation hub. From this surface, users can move into Chat Room, Training, Dog Space, History, notifications, profile, and device settings without losing the sense that the dog is the center of the service.

Surface UX role Primary transition
Live Avatar Detail Shows the dog's visible state, emotion, and care context in a more intuitive way than raw metrics. Tap or inspect the avatar to reveal status detail and quick actions.
Chat Preview Surfaces the latest interpreted message so users understand why the dog may be acting a certain way. Open Chat Room for full dialogue history and response actions.
Quick Action Lets owners respond quickly to needs such as walk, wait, stop, training, or care prompts. Trigger an action, start a related flow, or close a resolved scenario.
Training entry Connects current behavior or scheduled practice to the learning system. Move from Care Home into Training Journey or Practice Player.
Utility access Keeps profile, notification, and device controls reachable without turning the home screen into a settings page. Open dog profile, alerts, collar connection, battery, or account settings.
Push and deep link Allows urgent events to bring users directly back to the relevant dog context. Land on Care Home, Chat Room, History, or Dog Space depending on the event.

Dog Space Structure

Dog Space was planned as the dog's own environment inside the app. While Care Home focuses on immediate status and response, Dog Space supports longer engagement through a room view, avatar presence, inventory, decoration, rewards, and return paths from other parts of the service.

The structure needed to feel playful without becoming detached from the product purpose. Customization, item use, and room decoration were connected back to care, walking rewards, training progress, and repeated visits.

Area Purpose Connected flow
3D Room Viewer Gives the dog a persistent place where users can see the avatar and room state. Enter from Care Home, History rewards, push links, or item events.
Real-time avatar Keeps the character present in the room while reflecting live or recent dog context. Syncs back to Care Home and Chat Room when the dog state changes.
Edit Mode Lets users decorate the space and place items without breaking the core navigation. Connects to Inventory and Shop, then returns to Dog Space.
Inventory and Shop Supports consumables, custom skins, room items, and reward-based collection. Items can be earned from walks, training, records, or purchased as custom assets.
Return and recovery Defines how users close Dog Space, recover from blocked states, or return after an external event. Close returns to Care Home; errors route to a clear recovery UI.

Map and Training Systems

The app experience also extended beyond communication. I explored the map as a pet lifestyle platform that could combine walking records, safety context, community information, and gamified motivation.

Training was structured with references from leading dog training apps and learning products. The goal was to move from loose training ideas into a curriculum that could support basic obedience, manners, socialization, behavior challenges, and care routines.

System Direction Example features
Map insight Turn walks into behavior patterns, not just paths. Behavior heatmap, sniff index, stress zones, walk report
Safety layer Help owners understand nearby risks during outdoor activity. Safety pins, danger alerts, privacy zone, status badge
Retention loop Make walking feel rewarding and repeatable. Memory map, territory overlay, treasure hunt, shareable result card
Training curriculum Structure learning from foundational commands to advanced care and behavior support. Name recognition, recall, leash walking, socialization, alone training

Pre Order Website and Funnel

The pre order website was not only a product introduction page. It needed to define a new category, explain why the product matters, answer trust questions, and collect measurable demand.

I considered two visual directions: a warmer editorial story that builds trust through lifestyle context, and a more technical commerce system focused on options, specs, pricing, and benefit quantification. For an early pet hardware service, the stronger direction was to lead with trust, emotion, and scenario based explanation, while still supporting clear conversion.

Funnel layer Design decision Why it mattered
Hero and story Lead with owner uncertainty and the value of understanding the dog. Make the new product category emotionally legible in the first screen.
Lead form Collect email, region, age group, dog breed, and optional profile fields. Support demand capture and future segmentation.
Analytics Plan UTM preservation, GA client ID, lead events, and submit success or failure states. Make the pre order funnel measurable after launch.
FAQ and policy Address shipping, refunds, subscriptions, hardware fit, warranty, coverage, privacy, and support. Reduce purchase anxiety for an early hardware and AI service.

Launch Outcome

The project moved from concept level UX into a structured product and launch experience. The final case is not only about creating screens, but about defining how a smart collar service should explain itself, behave under uncertainty, and guide owners toward useful action.

  • Defined BeroAI as an AI IoT communication service rather than a simple pet tracker.
  • Structured the mobile app around Care Home, Chat Room, Dog Space, Map, Training, History, and utility states.
  • Translated collar signals into contextual owner guidance through AI communication scenarios.
  • Created trust rules for sensor confidence, permissions, device pairing, offline states, and no data cases.
  • Designed the app experience directly, including avatar character direction, UI screen structure, user flows, onboarding branches, and core scenario paths.
  • Planned a pre order website with product storytelling, lead capture, analytics events, and risk reducing FAQ content.

Reflection

This project reinforced that AI product design is not about making intelligence visible for its own sake. It is about helping people understand uncertainty, decide what to do, and trust the limits of the system.

For BeroAI, the most important design move was to connect hardware signals, AI interpretation, app experience, and pre order communication into one coherent service. The collar collects signals, but the product value comes from turning those signals into context, care, training, and a stronger relationship between dogs and owners.
