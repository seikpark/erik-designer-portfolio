AIBeroAI · AI IoT Product by Erik Park · 2025 Q4 - 2026 Q2

An AI IoT service that connects dogs and owners through two way communication.

Powered by a smart collar and mobile app, BeroAI helps owners understand dog behaviors, support training, and build better communication through gamified interactions.

EPErik ParkProduct Strategy / Service Planning / UX Design

Overview

BeroAI is an AI IoT pet communication service designed to help dog owners better understand their dogs through a smart collar and mobile app experience.

In this project, I worked across service planning, product strategy, mobile app UX structure, product messaging, and pre order website launch. My focus was to connect the smart collar, AI based interpretation, app based insights, and web conversion flow into one coherent product journey.

The core design challenge was not simply to present data collected from a device. The goal was to translate complex signals into clear, understandable, and actionable experiences for dog owners.

I helped shape BeroAI from an AI IoT product concept into a user centered service experience by connecting the smart collar, mobile app UX, product messaging, and pre order website launch.

Problem

Dog owners often struggle to understand their dogs' behavioral changes, emotional signals, stress patterns, and training needs in everyday situations.

Many pet technology products focus on measurable data such as location, activity, or health indicators. However, what dog owners often want to understand is more practical and emotional.

  • Is my dog okay right now?
  • Why is my dog behaving this way?
  • What should I do next?

For BeroAI, the core product problem was to turn signals collected through a smart collar and interpreted through AI into an experience that felt clear, trustworthy, and useful for everyday dog owners.

The product also needed a pre order website that could explain the value of BeroAI quickly and guide interested users toward action.

Role & Constraints

My role was to translate BeroAI's technical product direction into a user centered service experience.

I focused on making sure the smart collar, AI interpretation, mobile app, and pre order website did not feel like separate pieces. Instead, they needed to work together as one continuous journey from product understanding to usage and pre order conversion.

  • Product Strategy - Defined BeroAI as an AI IoT communication service, not just a smart collar.
  • Service Planning - Structured the flow from smart collar signal collection to AI interpretation, mobile app insights, and owner action.
  • Mobile App UX Structure - Organized the app around dog status, behavior insights, training recommendations, records, and gamified engagement.
  • Product Messaging - Refined the narrative around helping owners understand their dogs better without overclaiming that the product can perfectly translate dogs.
  • Pre Order Website Launch - Worked on the product story, page structure, key value communication, and CTA flow.

The main constraints were the early product stage, trust in AI communication, hardware and software integration, and the need to explain a market facing product clearly before mature long term usage metrics were available.

Product Positioning

BeroAI is powered by a smart collar and AI technology, but the user value is not the technology itself.

For dog owners, the value is being able to understand their dog more easily, make better decisions, and build a stronger relationship through care and training.

Instead of positioning the product as a device that perfectly translates dogs, I framed BeroAI as an AI communicator that helps owners better understand behavior and context signals.

  • Understand your dog better.
  • Turn behavior signals into actionable insights.
  • A smarter way to care, train, and bond with your dog.

Mobile App Experience

The mobile app was designed as more than a dashboard. Its purpose was to help owners understand their dogs and decide what to do next.

  • Understand Loop - Helps owners quickly understand their dog's current status, behavior changes, and possible emotional signals through summary cards, behavior insights, and clear status messages.
  • Respond Loop - Guides owners from insight to action with recommended actions, training suggestions, care guidance, and context based tips.
  • Bonding Loop - Supports repeat engagement through training missions, achievement records, profile growth, and gamified interactions.

Service Flow

The service flow was structured to make sure the hardware and app experience worked together clearly.

  • Smart collar collects behavior and context signals.
  • AI interpretation turns signals into behavior insight.
  • The mobile app presents understandable guidance.
  • The owner responds through care, training, or daily action.
  • The loop supports a stronger owner dog bond over time.

Pre Order Website

The pre order website was designed as a market facing entry point for the product.

The goal was not only to explain what BeroAI is, but also to help users understand why it matters and how it can fit into their relationship with their dog.

  • Hero - Communicated the core value and positioning from the first screen.
  • Problem - Presented the everyday challenge of understanding dog behavior and emotional signals.
  • Product - Explained how the smart collar and mobile app work together.
  • Key Benefits - Organized value around dog status, behavior insights, training support, and bonding.
  • How It Works - Explained the flow from sensing to AI interpretation, app insight, and owner action.
  • Pre Order CTA - Created a clear path from product understanding to pre order action.

Launch Outcome

The project resulted in a defined AI IoT service experience, a structured mobile app UX framework, product messaging, and a launched pre order website.

  • BeroAI was framed as an AI IoT communication service for dog owners, not just a smart collar or pet wearable.
  • The app experience was structured around dog status, behavior insights, training recommendations, records, and gamified engagement.
  • The service flow connected device signals to mobile insights and practical owner actions.
  • AI and IoT were translated into owner centered value: understanding, care, training, and bonding.
  • The pre order website launched with product story, key feature communication, how it works flow, and CTA structure.

The project moved beyond concept level UX and reached a real pre order website launch, turning a technically complex AI IoT product into a clearer user facing service experience.

Reflection

This project reinforced that AI product design is not only about showing intelligence. It is about helping users understand, trust, and act on that intelligence.

For a product concept like dog communication, the experience needed to be careful with its claims. Instead of presenting AI as something that can perfectly translate dogs, the service needed to focus on contextual insights, behavior patterns, and practical guidance.

The project also showed the importance of connecting hardware, software, and web communication into one product journey. The smart collar, mobile app, and pre order site each had a different role, but they needed to communicate the same value clearly.
