PDIn Product Redesign by Erik Park · 2023

Product simplification and retention redesign

Simplified a complex product experience while improving team workflows, delivery clarity, and key landing and home screen journeys.

EPErik ParkProduct Owner / Design Lead
+40%

Overview

Butler needed to make a complex service more accessible to general users while improving internal task management and documentation.

Challenge

The product experience had to become easier to understand, and the team needed clearer workflows to support product delivery.

Approach

  • Simplified the product through direct UX improvements for general users.
  • Redesigned the landing and home screen to improve the first product experience.
  • Implemented Jira and Notion to improve task management, documentation, and team workflows.
  • Developed a hybrid Waterfall process for improved delivery clarity.

Outcome

The landing and home screen redesign increased user retention by 40% and gave the team a clearer delivery process.
