Digital agency division and delivery system

Overview

Rise Partners required a digital delivery function that could connect proposals, client pipelines, design leadership, and cross-functional execution.

Challenge

The team needed clearer operating rhythms for digital work while maintaining delivery quality and strengthening strategic client conversations.

Approach

  • Established and led a digital agency division across business modeling, branding, and internal workflows.
  • Directed cross-functional teams for mobile and web app development.
  • Led IT B2B proposals to expand client pipelines through sales engagement and strategic pitching.

Outcome

The work created a clearer delivery system for digital projects and supported stronger proposal-led growth for client opportunities.
