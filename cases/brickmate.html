Overview

Brickmate's global client work required design leadership that could maintain creative quality while improving team operations and delivery consistency.

Challenge

Multiple high-value client projects needed consistent design direction, reliable team workflows, and scalable onboarding and training processes.

Approach

  • Managed $1.2M+ in product deliveries for Samsung, SM Entertainment, Lotte, and Danal.
  • Led key design concept development and set visual direction across major projects.
  • Built team-wide onboarding, hiring, and training processes to improve creative consistency.
  • Played a key role in the company's $10M M&A through design operations and workflow standardization.

Outcome

The work strengthened delivery quality and created repeatable design operations that supported larger company-level business outcomes.
